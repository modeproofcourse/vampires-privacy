VAMPIRES! – Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Developer: Martin Gofroj

What the game processes

The game requires no sign-up or login and does not itself collect your name, e-mail, location or contacts (for data processed by Google's ad service, see Ads below). For online leaderboards and friends to work, it sends the following data to the server:

  • a random player ID and a secret key generated on first launch (not linked to you as a person or to your device; the server stores only a hash of the key),
  • the nickname you choose in the game (the default is “Vampire” with a random number),
  • game results: score, run length, number of kills, chosen character and time of play,
  • an invite code and who invited you to the game,
  • friends, challenges and messages: your friends list, challenges to friends with their results and pre-written messages for challenges (the game sends no free text),
  • reports and blocks: whom you report and why, and whom you block. Only the game admin sees reports; the reported player is not told about them,
  • a notification token (only if you allow notifications): a random identifier of the app on your phone used to send you notifications about friends' challenges. Notifications are delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; the token is used only for this, together with the game's language so that notifications arrive in your language. It is deleted when you delete your online account; if you uninstall the game, the token may subsequently become invalid, and we remove it from the server once the service reports that it is no longer valid.

The server also remembers when the game was last online and uses it to show other players on the leaderboard and in friend lists whether you are in the game right now. From completed runs, the server calculates your player level (experience) and totals your nights survived and time played; these are shown next to your portrait and on your player card. Your nickname and results are publicly visible on leaderboards and in other players' friend lists. So do not put your real name or any other personal data in your nickname.

Game progress, settings and your own results are stored on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the game also backs up your progress to Saved Games in your Google account so that it can be restored after reinstalling or on a new phone. Game data may also be included in Android's system backup (if you have it turned on on your phone). Both backups are managed by Google; we have no access to your Google account.

Bug reports. When you send a bug report in the game, the developer receives by email, through the FormSubmit service, your text, nickname, player ID, number of games played, selected character, device details (model, system and game version, processor, graphics, screen, language) and the game log. Only what you confirm with the Send button is sent.

Invites through Google Play. If you install the game through a friend's link, the game reads the invite code in that link from the Google Play Install Referrer service so it can connect you with the person who invited you. It uses no other install data.

Where the data is stored

Online data is stored in a database of the Supabase service in the European Union (Ireland). The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). We do not sell online data or use it for advertising or tracking. Supabase and its subprocessors process it to provide data storage.

Supabase primarily provides its services through a Singapore-based company with a US affiliate; Google provides analytics, notifications, ads and Google Play Games. Some data may therefore also be processed outside the EEA, particularly in Singapore and the USA. Depending on the service, transfers to countries without an adequate level of data protection rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Transfers to Google LLC in the USA may also rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. You can contact us at the e-mail address below for information on the safeguards used and how to obtain a copy.

Gameplay statistics

To learn where the game is too hard and what players enjoy, the game sends gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example the start and end of a run, chosen upgrades, purchases with in-game currency, rewarded ads watched and screens opened, together with device information (model, system and game version, country derived from the IP address) and a random app instance ID. The statistics are not linked to your nickname or player ID (they are tied only to the random app instance ID), do not use the advertising ID and are not linked to ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland they are sent only with your consent (in the same dialog as for ads; you can change it in Settings → Privacy). Google keeps them for at most 14 months.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded ads (for example a revive or double gold) – an ad plays only when you tap it. Ads are served by Google AdMob, a Google service that may process your device's advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device information, ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic data for this purpose. This is used to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and – if you consent – personalize ads. For details see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent on first launch. You can change your choice at any time in the game in Settings → Privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings under Ads (depending on your Android version, you will find it under Privacy or under Google).

Why we process data

  • Online features (player ID, nickname, results, level, leaderboards, friends, challenges and messages): to provide you with the game features you use (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Leaderboards, friends and challenges cannot work without the data needed for an online account.
  • Reports, blocks, nickname checks and protection against cheating: our legitimate interest in a safe and fair game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Notifications: your consent to receiving them and to the related processing of the token (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can withdraw this consent by turning off notifications on your phone.
  • Gameplay statistics and personalized ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can change at any time in Settings → Privacy. Non-personalized ads and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding and protecting the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), where the processing does not require consent. Where consent is needed to access information on your device, we ask for it. Ads play only when you tap them.
  • Bug reports: our legitimate interest in fixing bugs in the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). A report is sent only when you send it.

How long we keep data

  • Online account (nickname, results, level, friends, blocks): until you delete the account (or until the game ends its online services). We also remove a notification token earlier if FCM reports that it is invalid.
  • Challenges and messages: completed challenges 90 days; all others 14 days.
  • Reports: 12 months from the latest report.
  • Gameplay statistics: at most 14 months at Google.
  • Bug reports: in the developer's email for up to 12 months.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw your consent at any time (in Settings → Privacy or by turning off notifications); this does not affect processing that took place before. To exercise your rights, e-mail us at the address below – include your nickname and the invite code from Friends so that we can find your data. We will respond within one month.

If you believe we are mishandling your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Czech data protection authority (Office for Personal Data Protection) or with the data protection authority in your country.

Players in the USA

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut or Utah), you also have these rights:

  • Opt out of targeted advertising and the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. Google AdMob may, however, use your advertising ID and device information for targeted ads, which some state laws treat as sharing. You can opt out in the game in Settings → Privacy, or by turning off ad personalization in your phone settings.
  • Know what data we have about you, have it corrected or deleted. E-mail us at the address below. We will respond within 45 days.
  • No discrimination. If you exercise your rights, the game will work for you just the same.

Categories of data collected: identifiers (random player ID, app instance ID, advertising ID), nickname, in-game activity (including gameplay statistics) and ad interactions, approximate location derived from the IP address, and diagnostic data. Purposes and recipients are described above.

Payments

In-game purchases are handled by Google Play. Only Google sees your payment details (card, address); neither the game nor the developer receives them. The game receives a purchase confirmation (purchase identifier and the product bought) and stores it on your device so that each purchase is credited only once. Gameplay statistics may record which product was bought and at what price, without any payment details.

Deleting your data

You can delete your online account with all online data (nickname, results, level, friends, challenges, messages, reports and blocks) in the game: Settings → Delete online account. It is deleted immediately and permanently. Without the game, you can request deletion by e-mail at the address below (include your nickname and the invite code from Friends) – we will delete it within 30 days. Step by step: Delete your account. Uninstalling the game deletes the data stored on the device (backups in your Google account can be deleted in your Google settings).
