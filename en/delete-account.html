VAMPIRES! – Delete your account

VAMPIRES! game · Developer: Martin Gofroj

In the game

  1. In the main menu, tap the gear (Settings).
  2. At the bottom right, tap Delete online account.
  3. Confirm with the Delete account button. The account is deleted immediately.

Without the game (by e-mail)

Send an e-mail to imthetruemartin@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion” and include your nickname and invite code (in the game: Friends → Your code). We will delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Permanently and immediately: nickname, leaderboard results, player level, nights survived and time played, friends and invites, challenges and messages, reports and blocks, the notification token and the random player ID. We keep none of it.

What remains

  • Game progress stored on your phone – gold, rubies, vampires, the Crypt (deleted by uninstalling the game) – and its backup in Google Play Saved Games (delete it in your Google account settings). Your player level starts again at 1.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics in Google Analytics for Firebase – they are not linked to your nickname or player ID, and Google deletes them after 14 months at the latest.
  • Data processed by the Google AdMob ad service – governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
