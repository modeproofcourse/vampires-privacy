VAMPIRES! – Community Rules

Effective November 2, 2026 · Developer: Martin Gofroj

Other players see your nickname, results and challenge messages. By choosing a nickname you agree to these rules.

Not allowed

  • Your nickname must not be vulgar, offensive, sexual, violent or hateful (towards nationality, race, religion, gender, sexual orientation or disability).
  • Do not pretend to be the game team, the game admin or any other real person.
  • Do not put personal data (real name, phone number, e-mail, address), links or advertising in your nickname.
  • Do not cheat: do not modify the game, submit fake results or exploit bugs in the game.
  • Do not harass other players or abuse reporting.

Reporting and blocking

To report a player, tap the flag on their player card on the Leaderboard, in Friends or in a challenge. Only the game admin sees reports; the reported player is not told about them. With the Block button you stop receiving challenges, messages and notifications from that player; you can unblock them in Friends.

What happens if the rules are broken

The game admin may replace the nickname with a substitute one (the original can no longer be used), delete results from leaderboards, remove the player from leaderboards or delete their online account. The game and the server reject inappropriate nicknames as soon as they are chosen.
